# K2: localising the point of no return

Pre-registered 2026-10-25, before any measurement. Protocol: `prereg/common.md`.

## Data
- RoboLab certified one-way clips from host 84, with certificate frame t*. K2 does not run without them.
- Clips are excluded and logged if t* falls outside the frames where a(t) is defined, or if the clip fails a filter.
- Ego4D PNR frames: the same test with the same thresholds, run once the licence arrives and reported as a separate row.

## Native-frame timing
At stride 3 a tubelet spans 6 native frames. To recover finer timing with the same training-matched inputs:
1. Run three stride-3 streams at native-frame offsets 0, 1 and 2.
2. Place each stream's normalised a(j) at its tubelet's centre native frame.
3. Merge the three streams into one series a(t) on native frames. Each stream is normalised by the median e_f pooled over all three streams.

With offsets 0–2, the merged series has a value at three of every six native frames, so the gap between values is at most 4 native frames, inside the ±8 tolerance.

## Predictions, for each model and clip
- **Primary:** t̂ = native frame of the argmax of the merged normalised a(t).
- Secondary: the first tubelet where a(j) exceeds the clip's own 95th percentile of a.

## Baselines
- Flow: argmax of the native-frame flow f(t) over the same defined range.
- Chance: the probability that a uniformly random frame in the defined range lands within ±8 frames of t*, computed for each clip and averaged. It is reported both overall and by clip-length bin: <60, 60–120 and >120 frames.

## Metric
Hit rate = the fraction of clips with |t̂ − t*| ≤ 8 frames (0.53 s at 15 Hz), with a bootstrap CI over clips.

## Pass rule
Evaluated with the 8-in / 2-out window on the merged stride-3 series.

**Pass** if both hold:
- hit rate(a) > 60 %
- hit rate(flow) < 25 %

The chance rate is reported beside both. **Fail** otherwise.

LTX-Video is reported under the same rule as the non-JEPA row, at its 8-frame latent resolution. It does not count toward the project-level pass.

K2 passes or fails on the primary model, V-JEPA 2 ViT-g. ViT-L and V-JEPA 2.1 are replications, reported beside it under the same rule.

## Reported regardless of outcome
- The full distribution of t̂ − t* in frames, as a histogram and quantiles.
- The share of clips where the peak comes before t* (lead) versus after it (lag), with a CI.
- The same metrics for the secondary prediction, for raw errors, for offset 0 alone (6-frame resolution), for stride 1, and for the 16-in / 4-out window.
